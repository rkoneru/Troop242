## 2025-05-15 - Scope management for micro-UX
**Learning:** Large structural refactors (like adding GlobalLayout and <main> landmarks) often exceed the 50-line micro-UX limit and can have unintended side effects like massive lockfile updates if dependencies are re-resolved.
**Action:** Prioritize single, highly-targeted accessibility fixes (e.g., ARIA labels) that deliver immediate value while strictly adhering to line count constraints.
