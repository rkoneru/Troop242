## 2025-05-15 - Improving Login Form Accessibility and UX
**Learning:** Explicit association between labels and inputs using `htmlFor` and `id` is crucial for screen reader accessibility, even when labels are visually adjacent. Additionally, a password visibility toggle is a high-impact micro-UX pattern that reduces user frustration during authentication.
**Action:** Always ensure `htmlFor` and `id` are used in forms, and provide visibility toggles for sensitive inputs like passwords.
