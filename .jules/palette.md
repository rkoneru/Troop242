## 2025-05-18 - [Accessible Interactive Onboarding & FAQ Components]
**Learning:** Interactive onboarding checklist items, category tab selectors, and expandable FAQ accordions created with generic `<div>` or `<button>` elements without explicit WAI-ARIA roles (`role="checkbox"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-controls`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-selected`) and keyboard event listeners (`Enter`/`Space`) are inaccessible to screen reader users and keyboard-only navigation.
**Action:** Always provide `role="checkbox"`, `tabIndex={0}`, `aria-checked`, `aria-label`, and `onKeyDown` handlers for custom interactive checklist items; use `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, and `role="tabpanel"` for tabbed views; and attach `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `id`, and `role="region"` for expandable accordion sections.
